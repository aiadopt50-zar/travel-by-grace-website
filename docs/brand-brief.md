# Travel By Grace — Approved Brand Direction

## Working logo direction

**Concept 2: Triber Route**

The visual identity uses a simplified white compact MPV on a flowing coastal route. This direction was chosen because the vehicle makes the brand feel more personal and directly connected to Lucille's service.

## Brand wording

- Business name: **Travel By Grace**
- Marketing tagline: **Your Journey. My Personal Care.**
- Descriptor: **Personalised adventures, thoughtfully arranged by Lucille du Toit.**

The marketing tagline is used selectively rather than being permanently attached to every logo lockup.

## Personality

- Elegant and calm
- Personal and approachable
- Owner-operated
- Coastal and locally rooted
- Subtle faith influence, without overt religious imagery

## Logo system

- Full illustrated vehicle logo for hero sections and marketing
- Simplified vehicle mark for favicon, WhatsApp and small-format use
- Temporary script-style treatment for Lucille's signature until an authentic signature scan is provided
