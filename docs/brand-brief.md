# Travel By Grace — Stage 1 Brand Brief

## Confirmed identity

**Business name:** Travel By Grace  
**Owner-driver:** Lucille du Toit  
**Base:** Klein Brak River, Western Cape  
**Marketing slogan:** Your Journey. My Personal Care.  
**Positioning line:** Personally owned, driven and hosted by Lucille du Toit.

The slogan is reserved for selected marketing material rather than being permanently attached beneath the primary logo.

## Brand personality

Travel By Grace should feel elegant, calm, warm, trustworthy and personal. It must clearly present Lucille as the sole owner, driver, host and primary point of contact. Copy should use a first-person voice wherever natural.

Avoid corporate language such as “our team”, “our drivers” or “one of our consultants”.

## Audience

The initial focus is local residents who need reliable, personalised transport or would enjoy a customised short getaway.

## Services at launch

- Airport transfers
- Customised Cape Town weekend getaways
- Local wine-tasting trips
- Personalised outings and travel ideas based on the customer’s preferences

## Vehicle and capacity

- White 2023 Renault Triber
- Recommended public wording: “Comfortable private travel for up to five passengers.”
- A sixth passenger should only be accepted where seating, luggage, comfort, licensing and insurance requirements allow it.

## Colour direction

Primary colours:

- Coastal Turquoise — `#159A9C`
- Deep Lagoon — `#0B5960`
- Soft Seafoam — `#DDF3F0`
- Warm Sand — `#E9DCC4`
- Shell White — `#FAF8F4`
- Charcoal — `#263238`

Turquoise is the hero colour. Deep Lagoon provides contrast. Warm Sand prevents the identity from feeling cold or clinical.

## Typography direction

- Headings: an elegant, readable serif such as Cormorant Garamond or Lora
- Body and interface text: a clean sans-serif such as Inter, Source Sans 3 or a reliable system font stack
- Lucille’s signature: supplied handwritten signature converted to a clean transparent digital mark

## Logo concepts

### Concept 1 — Graceful Wing (strong recommendation)

A refined single-line wing that also suggests a coastal wave and forward movement. A subtle negative-space detail may hint at faith without becoming an obvious religious emblem. The mark should work in one colour and remain clear at favicon size.

Why this is strongest: it represents grace, care, freedom and travel without making the business look like an airline or generic shuttle company.

### Concept 2 — Triber Journey

A simplified side or three-quarter silhouette inspired by Lucille’s white Renault Triber, paired with a flowing coastal road. The vehicle must be stylised rather than drawn like a dealership illustration.

Why it works: it immediately communicates personal road transport and makes the service tangible.

Risk: the identity may feel tied to one vehicle model and could date faster if the vehicle changes.

### Concept 3 — Coastal Signature

A flowing route line becomes a wave, then resolves into Lucille’s handwritten signature. The business name sits beneath it in an elegant serif.

Why it works: it is highly personal and reinforces that customers deal directly with Lucille.

Risk: the signature must remain legible and should not expose a signature used for banking or legal documents. A dedicated brand signature is preferable.

## Photography

Use authentic photographs of Lucille, the white Renault Triber and real Garden Route locations. Prioritise clear, well-lit images with welcoming expressions. Avoid heavy filters and generic stock imagery where authentic images are available.

## Voice examples

Preferred:

- “I’ll help you plan a journey that suits your pace.”
- “From airport collection to a relaxed weekend away, you deal directly with me.”
- “Got an idea? Tell me what you have in mind.”

Avoid:

- “Our fleet provides comprehensive transportation solutions.”
- “Contact one of our consultants.”
