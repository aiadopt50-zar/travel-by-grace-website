# Travel By Grace replacement website

## Direction
- Fun road trips with a touch of luxury.
- Cape Town entertainment weekends are the strongest conversion theme, while Garden Route services remain clearly represented.
- The experience is sold as one coordinated trip: private travel, accommodation ideas and itinerary planning.
- The vehicle is described by comfort and practicality rather than model naming.
- Purple is the core brand colour, supported by sunset gold, pink and coastal blue.

## Motion
- A short vehicle arrival scene plays only once per browser session.
- Desktop visitors see a subtle scroll-progress vehicle and animated route map.
- Mobile motion is simplified; reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Event content
The first event set covers September 2026 through January 2027 and is ordered by date. Event cards link to official venue listings. Dates and availability must be reviewed regularly because they are controlled by third parties.

## Images
Real supplied photographs are used for the vehicle, interior, luggage space, coast, accommodation and lifestyle sections. The portrait receives restrained CSS brightening and contrast treatment without changing Lucille's appearance.

## Marketing
`public/assets/images/share-card.svg` is the 1200 × 630 Open Graph marketing visual used when the website is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook and other social platforms.

## Deployment
The existing `.cpanel.yml` deployment remains suitable. After review and approval, merge this replacement branch into `main` and deploy through the current cPanel Git workflow.
