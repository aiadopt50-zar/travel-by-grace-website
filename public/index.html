Meet Lucille

A familiar face from your first message to your final stop

Hi, I’m Lucille du Toit, the owner and driver behind Travel By Grace. I enjoy helping people turn a simple trip into something easy, comfortable and genuinely personal.

Based in Klein Brak River, I serve local residents and visitors across the Mossel Bay and George area. I also arrange longer custom journeys as far as Cape Town when booked in advance.

Because I operate independently, bookings are subject to my availability. The benefit is that you always know who you are speaking to and who will be looking after your journey.

Comfortable private travel for up to five passengers. A sixth passenger may be accommodated depending on luggage, seating requirements and the nature of the journey.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure about something? WhatsApp or call Lucille directly.

How far in advance should I book?

Two days’ notice is preferred. Earlier booking is recommended for weekends, Cape Town trips, events and journeys involving accommodation or activity arrangements.

How are prices calculated?

Most journeys are quoted individually according to distance, timing, waiting time, passengers and any arrangements made on your behalf. Airport transfer prices will be added to the website later.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Cash or instant EFT. A deposit may be required to secure a booking, with the balance due as agreed directly with Lucille.

Can you arrange accommodation and activities?

Yes, depending on the journey and your requirements. Any third-party booking terms or costs will be explained before confirmation.

Can I bring a child seat or pet?

Child seats may be available by prior request. Pets are considered by prior arrangement. Please mention both when first enquiring.

Is Cape Town Airport included?

George Airport is the primary airport service. Cape Town Airport may be included as part of a specially arranged Cape Town journey.
