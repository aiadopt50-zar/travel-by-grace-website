A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Before we go.

Is this a fixed package?

Each trip is shaped around your group, dates and preferences. The trip stories share past experiences; they are inspiration for your own plan, not promises of a fixed itinerary.

Can Lucille help with accommodation and shows?

Lucille can help coordinate accommodation ideas and selected experiences. Third-party prices, availability and booking terms are checked and discussed before anything is confirmed.

How do quotes and deposits work?

Pricing is provided for your particular journey. Any deposit, balance and payment arrangements are agreed directly with Lucille.

Can I arrange just an airport transfer?

Yes. George Airport transfers and local private travel are available by prior arrangement. Share your route, passengers and luggage requirements when enquiring.

What about children, pets or extra luggage?

Please discuss child seats, pets and luggage when you first enquire. Suitability and space are confirmed for your specific journey.
