TRAVEL BY GRACE

Booking terms

Every journey is arranged personally with Lucille du Toit. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Confirming your journey

Dates, collection points, destinations, passengers, luggage and any special requirements must be agreed with Lucille. All journeys are subject to availability and prior arrangement.

Quotes and payment

A quote is prepared for your journey. Any deposit, final balance, payment method and cancellation arrangements are discussed directly before confirmation.

Accommodation, events and activities

Third-party services are subject to their own availability, prices and terms. Lucille can help coordinate ideas and selected arrangements, but third-party availability is not guaranteed by this website.

Trip stories and inspiration

Completed-trip stories describe past experiences. They are not fixed packages or offers for future event dates. Photo illustrations used in the travel-inspiration collection represent possible journey ideas.

Discuss the details
